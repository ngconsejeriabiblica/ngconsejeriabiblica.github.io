The Transformation

True change begins in a relationship with God

Biblical counseling starts from the premise that the Bible is the Word of God (2 Timothy 3:16-17) and is entirely sufficient to meet humanity's mental, spiritual, and emotional needs (2 Peter 1:3-4). Through it, we are taught, reproved, corrected, and trained in righteousness (2 Timothy 3:16).

The transformation essential to any lasting change in a person begins with a relationship with God. That change in the vertical relationship then produces change in how we relate to ourselves and to others.

"Do not conform to the pattern of this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you may test and approve what God's will is—his good, pleasing and perfect will."

Romans 12:2
